| **Genre** | Animation / Comedy / Adventure |
| **Release Date** | November 2026 |

## 🎥 Family Movie Night Tips

- **Set the mood** – Dim lights, cozy blankets, popcorn
- **No phones** – Make it a screen-free bonding time
- **Discuss afterward** – Ask everyone their favorite parts
- **Make it a tradition** – Weekly family movie night
- **Rotate pickers** – Let each family member choose one week
