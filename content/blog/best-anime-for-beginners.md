## 💡 Tips for Starting Your Anime Journey

### 1. Watch with Subtitles (Sub) or Dubbed (Dub)?
- **Sub (Japanese + English subtitles)** → More authentic, better voice acting.
- **Dub (English voice actors)** → Easier to follow, good for beginners.
- **Our recommendation:** Start with **dub** for your first series, then switch to **sub** as you get used to it.

### 2. Don't Binge Too Fast
Anime is meant to be savored. Watch 2-3 episodes a day and take time to absorb the story.

### 3. Explore Different Genres
Don't stick to one genre. Try action, then comedy, then drama. Anime is incredibly diverse.

### 4. Join the Community
Follow anime subreddits, join Discord servers, and discuss with other fans. It makes the experience much richer.
