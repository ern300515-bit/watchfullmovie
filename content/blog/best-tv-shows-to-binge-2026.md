## 💡 Tips for Binge-Watching

- **Set a schedule** – Try a one-episode-per-day rule to savor the experience
- **Use subtitles** – You might miss important dialogue
- **Take breaks** – Give your eyes and mind a rest
- **Avoid spoilers** – Stay off social media if you're behind
- **Discuss it** – Join fan forums or watch parties
