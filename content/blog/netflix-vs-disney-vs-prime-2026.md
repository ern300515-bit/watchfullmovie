## 🎬 Deep Dive: Netflix

### Overview

Netflix is the undisputed king of streaming. With over 260 million subscribers worldwide, it's the most popular platform by a wide margin. Known for its massive library and prolific original content, Netflix has something for everyone.

### Content Library

| Category | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|----------|-----------|------------|
| **Movies** | Huge variety, frequent new releases | Content rotates regularly |
| **TV Series** | Iconic originals (Stranger Things, The Crown) | Some series get cancelled too early |
| **Documentaries** | Best in class (Our Planet, Tiger King) | Limited selection in some regions |
| **Reality TV** | Excellent (Love Is Blind, The Circle) | Not for everyone |
| **Kids' Content** | Decent selection | Less than Disney+ |
| **Anime** | Growing collection | Limited compared to Crunchyroll |

### Pricing Tiers (2026)

## 🎯 Which Service Should You Choose?

### Quick Decision Tree

1. **Do you have kids under 12?**
   - Yes → **Disney+** (must-have for families)
   - No → Go to question 2

2. **Are you already an Amazon Prime member?**
   - Yes → **Prime Video** (already included)
   - No → Go to question 3

3. **What do you watch most?**
   - Movies & documentaries → **Netflix**
   - Quality dramas & prestige content → **Netflix** or **Prime Video**
   - Family & franchise content → **Disney+**

4. **What's your budget?**
   - Under $10/month → **Disney+** ($7.99)
   - $10-$20/month → **Netflix Standard** ($15.49)
   - Over $20/month → **Netflix Premium** ($22.99)

## 💡 Smart Budget Combinations

| Budget | Combination | Why |
|--------|-------------|-----|
| **$15/month** | Disney+ ($7.99) + Prime Video ($8.99) | Best for families + Prime members |
| **$20/month** | Netflix Standard ($15.49) + Prime Video (if already Prime) | Best all-rounder |

| **$35/month** | Netflix Premium ($22.99) + Disney+ ($7.99) + Prime Video (included with Prime) | Ultimate package |

### Pro Tips
- **Cycle subscriptions** – Subscribe to one, binge, cancel, and switch
- **Use free trials** – Netflix (30 days), Prime Video (30 days)
- **Look for bundles** – Disney+ + Hulu + ESPN+ gives great value
- **Share accounts** – Family plans let you split costs
- **Student discounts** – Many platforms offer reduced rates
