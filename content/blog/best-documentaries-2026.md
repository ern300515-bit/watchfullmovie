### 1. Our Planet II (2026)

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Director** | Alastair Fothergill |
| **Narrator** | David Attenborough |
| **Platform** | Netflix |
| **Genre** | Nature / Wildlife |
| **Episodes** | 8 |

The sequel to the groundbreaking nature documentary. This series explores the world's most precious ecosystems and the extraordinary wildlife that call them home—and the challenges they face.

**Why you should watch:** It's visually breathtaking. Every frame is a masterpiece, and the storytelling is both educational and deeply moving. Attenborough's narration is as powerful as ever.
