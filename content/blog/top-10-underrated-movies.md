Not every great movie becomes a blockbuster. Some of the best films are quietly released, overshadowed by big franchises or poor marketing. But that doesn't mean they aren't worth your time.

Whether you're tired of mainstream superhero flicks or craving something fresh and original, this list of **underrated movies** will introduce you to storytelling at its finest. From indie dramas to genre-bending thrillers, these hidden gems deserve a spot on your watchlist.

## 🎬 Why You Should Watch Underrated Movies

- **Fresh storylines** – No formulaic plots or predictable endings
- **Great performances** – Often feature career-best acting
- **Unique perspectives** – Explore ideas and themes you won't see elsewhere

## 🏆 Top 10 Underrated Movies of All Time

### 1. The Tale (2018) – Drama
**Where to watch:** Hulu, Amazon Prime Video  
**Why it's underrated:** A haunting and deeply personal film about memory and trauma. Laura Dern delivers a career-defining performance.  
**Perfect for:** Fans of *Spotlight* or *The Act of Killing*.

---

### 2. The Endless (2017) – Sci-Fi / Thriller
**Where to watch:** Netflix, Prime Video  
**Why it's underrated:** A mind-bending indie sci-fi that cleverly blends mystery, horror, and existential dread. The Benson & Moorhead duo at their best.  
**Perfect for:** Fans of *Coherence* or *Primer*.

---

### 3. The Last Black Man in San Francisco (2019) – Drama
**Where to watch:** Amazon Prime Video, Hulu  
**Why it's underrated:** A visually stunning meditation on gentrification, identity, and home. Gorgeous cinematography and a powerful score.  
**Perfect for:** Fans of *Moonlight* or *If Beale Street Could Talk*.

---

### 4. Columbus (2017) – Drama
**Where to watch:** Criterion Channel, Kanopy  
**Why it's underrated:** A quiet, patient film that finds meaning in architecture and human connection. Haley Lu Richardson shines.  
**Perfect for:** Fans of *Lost in Translation* or *Paterson*.

---

### 5. The Death of Stalin (2017) – Comedy / History
**Where to watch:** Netflix, Prime Video  
**Why it's underrated:** A pitch-black political satire that's both hilarious and terrifying. Armando Iannucci's sharp dialogue is a masterclass.  
**Perfect for:** Fans of *Veep* or *In the Loop*.

---

### 6. The Fall (2006) – Adventure / Fantasy
**Where to watch:** Prime Video (rent/buy)  
**Why it's underrated:** A visual feast filmed across 28 countries. Breathtaking cinematography and a moving story about storytelling itself.  
**Perfect for:** Fans of *The Cell* or *The Fountain*.

---

### 7. The Vast of Night (2019) – Sci-Fi
**Where to watch:** Amazon Prime Video  
**Why it's underrated:** A low-budget, high-concept thriller set in 1950s New Mexico. Its use of radio waves and mystery is brilliantly executed.  
**Perfect for:** Fans of *The Twilight Zone* or *Close Encounters of the Third Kind*.

---

### 8. The Duke (2020) – Comedy / Drama
**Where to watch:** Netflix, Prime Video  
**Why it's underrated:** Jim Broadbent gives a warm, charming performance in this true story about a man who stole a Goya painting. Heartwarming and funny.  
**Perfect for:** Fans of *The Best Exotic Marigold Hotel*.

---

### 9. Blue Ruin (2013) – Thriller
**Where to watch:** Netflix, Hulu  
**Why it's underrated:** A gripping, stripped-down revenge thriller that feels incredibly real. No fancy tricks – just raw tension and compelling storytelling.  
**Perfect for:** Fans of *Green Room* or *No Country for Old Men*.

---

### 10. The Rover (2014) – Drama / Sci-Fi
**Where to watch:** Prime Video, Kanopy  
**Why it's underrated:** A grim, post-apocalyptic drama set in the Australian outback. Guy Pearce and Robert Pattinson deliver intense, unforgettable performances.  
**Perfect for:** Fans of *Mad Max* or *The Road*.
