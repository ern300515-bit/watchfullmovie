Movies are full of secrets. Behind every iconic scene, legendary performance, and memorable line, there's a story that never made it to the screen. Some of these facts are so surprising, they'll make you see your favorite films in a completely new light.

From improvised moments that became legendary to casting decisions that almost changed cinema history—here are 10 mind-blowing movie facts you probably didn't know.

### 1. "I'm the King of the World!" Was Improvised

| Fact Detail | Information |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Movie** | Titanic (1997) |
| **Scene** | Jack standing at the bow of the ship |
| **What happened** | Leonardo DiCaprio's iconic line wasn't in the script. He ad-libbed it on the spot. |
| **Why it worked** | James Cameron loved the spontaneity and kept it in the final cut. |

**The result:** One of the most memorable lines in cinema history—and a moment that perfectly captured Jack's free-spirited character.

**Where to watch:** Netflix (currently streaming)

### 2. The "I Am Your Father" Twist Was Kept Secret from Everyone

| Fact Detail | Information |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Movie** | The Empire Strikes Back (1980) |
| **Scene** | Darth Vader reveals his identity to Luke |
| **What happened** | Only three people knew the twist before release: George Lucas, Irvin Kershner, and Mark Hamill. |
| **The secret** | The script read "Obi-Wan killed your father" to prevent leaks. |

**The result:** One of the greatest plot twists in cinema history—and a moment that changed Star Wars forever.

**Where to watch:** Disney+

### 3. Tom Hanks Gave Up His Salary for Forrest Gump

| Fact Detail | Information |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Movie** | Forrest Gump (1994) |
| **What happened** | Tom Hanks took a percentage of the box office instead of a salary. |
| **The result** | The film made $678 million worldwide. Hanks made over $70 million. |
| **Bonus** | He also won the Academy Award for Best Actor. |

**The result:** One of the smartest financial decisions in Hollywood history.

**Where to watch:** Netflix / Prime Video
